    Open on sites

    Sites a code can be opened on: from the chip’s Sites, right-clicking a ▶ button, the right-click menu or the popup. Put a placeholder where the code goes:

        Quick links

        Sites or pages one click away in the popup’s Links tab. Drag links by their handle to reorder them or move them to another group, or use the ⋯ menus. Tip: use Add page in the popup while you’re on a page.

        Pop-up blocker

        On these sites and their subdomains, stop ad pop-ups and new ad tabs, and stop a script from sending the page to another site (the “video opens in a new tab, this tab goes to an ad” trick). Links you click on the same site still open; Ctrl/⌘-click or middle-click always opens a new tab.

        Tip: switch on Block pop-ups in the popup, then reload the page.

          Video tools

          Videos on every site get a small toolbar at the top right when you point at them or pause:

          • ⚡ Faster loadingDownloads several pieces of the video at once, for servers that send each piece slowly.
          • QualityA menu, when the video has more than one.
          • ⬇ DownloadSaves an MP4. Keep the tab open while it runs.
          • EN subsReads burned-in Chinese or Japanese subtitles on this computer and covers them with English. Size and background are below.

          Turned off on these sites

          No toolbar or faster loading on these sites and their subdomains. Tip: switch off Video tools in the popup while you’re on the site.

            Shortcuts

            Select a code, or put the caret on one in a text box, then:

            • Alt+Shift+J Open trailer
            • Alt+Shift+K Search javtrailers.com
            • Alt+Shift+L Open on all your “Open on” sites
            • Right-click Open trailer for “…”, Search trailers for “…” or Open “…” on
